Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026. This policy applies to Seven War Offline, developed by Almin Ombasic and Muamer Prasovic.

Data collected by the game

This version is an offline card game with no user accounts, advertising, analytics or in-app purchases. The game does not collect or share personal data and does not send gameplay data to our servers.

Local device data

Local app files remain on your device. You can remove local app data through Android app settings or by uninstalling the game. Any backups made by your device or operating system are governed by its settings and provider policies.

Email support

If you contact sevenwargame7@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request. Email delivery and storage are handled by email service providers. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle your request, subject to applicable retention obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Children’s privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support email, contact us to request deletion.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if our data practices change.
