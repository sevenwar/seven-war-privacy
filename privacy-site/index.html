Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026. This policy applies to Seven War, developed by Almin Ombasic and Muamer Prasovic. Contact: sevenwargame7@gmail.com.

Offline gameplay and advertising

Seven War is an offline card game with no player accounts or in-app purchases. Gameplay does not require an internet connection. When an internet connection and a ready advertisement are available, the Android version may show a Google AdMob advertisement after a completed match. An unavailable advertisement does not prevent playing.

Data handled by Google AdMob

The game integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on consent, device settings and ad-serving mode, Google may collect and share the device IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising ID, app set ID and other applicable device or account identifiers, interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, and diagnostic information such as performance and crashes. Google processes this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit. We do not operate player-account or gameplay-data servers.

Privacy choices

Where required, Google UMP displays a privacy message before advertising is enabled. You can revisit applicable choices through Rules > Privacy & Licenses > Ad Privacy Choices. Availability depends on your region and the consent requirements determined by Google. Advertising identifiers can also be reset or deleted in Android settings. Declining advertising consent does not block offline gameplay. Non-personalized or limited advertising may still be permitted depending on your choices and applicable requirements.

Local app data

Local app files and privacy preferences may be stored on your device. You can remove local app data through Android app settings or by uninstalling the game. Device backup handling is governed by your operating-system settings. Google handles advertising-data retention under its own policies; clearing this app does not necessarily delete data already processed by Google.

Email support

If you contact sevenwargame7@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you send. We use it to respond to your request. Email providers process delivery and storage. We retain support correspondence as needed to address your request and meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of correspondence or ask privacy questions.

Children

We do not provide player accounts or ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support email, contact us to request its deletion.
